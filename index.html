About Me

Hello, I'm Alisha Shafique.

I'm a mental health professional working as a neuropsychologist and therapist in practice, blending clinical understanding with spiritual healing to support individuals on their journey toward emotional and mental balance.

With over 9 years of experience in corporate management, I bring a unique mix of structure, empathy, and real-world insight into my work. I understand the pressures of modern life and the emotional complexities that often come with it, which allows me to hold space for my clients in a grounded and practical way.

My work is rooted in the belief that healing isn't just about managing symptoms — it's about understanding yourself at a deeper level: emotionally, mentally, and internally.

My Approach

Integrative, personalised, whole-person care

My approach is integrative and personalised, designed to support healing at the level of mind, body, and inner self. I combine evidence-based therapies — Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Dialectical Behavior Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Internal Family Systems, and trauma-informed care — with insights from neuropsychology, mindfulness, and nervous system regulation. This lets us not only understand your patterns, but also work on how your brain and body respond to stress, emotion, and past experience.

How We Work Together

A structured, flexible process

Four stages that support long-term transformation — not a rigid script, but a rhythm we move through together.

One

Awareness

We begin by understanding your thoughts, emotional patterns, and behavioural responses — including how your past experiences and brain processes influence your present.

Two

Regulation

You'll learn practical tools through mindfulness and nervous system work to manage emotional intensity and move out of survival responses like anxiety, overthinking, or shutdown.

Three

Processing

We gently work through deeper layers — past experiences, trauma, core beliefs, and inner conflicts — using trauma-informed therapy, parts work (IFS), and hypnotherapy where needed.

Four

Integration

We focus on creating lasting change: healthier coping patterns, emotional resilience, and a more aligned, grounded version of yourself.

FAQs

Good to know before you book

Are you a licensed therapist?

I'm a mental health professional working as a neuropsychologist and therapist in practice, within my current qualifications and scope of practice. I'm not yet a licensed psychotherapist, and sessions are offered as psychoeducation, mental wellness support, and coaching rather than clinical psychotherapy or diagnosis.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes, subject to the legal and ethical limits explained in the consent section of the intake form — including risk of serious harm or a legal obligation such as a court order.

Are sessions online?

Yes, all sessions are conducted securely online via video call, so you can join from a private space wherever you are.

How long is each session?

Most sessions run between 45 and 60 minutes, depending on the type of session you choose.

How is spirituality involved in sessions?

Alongside evidence-based therapies, I draw on spiritually aligned and energy-based practices where it feels relevant to you. This is always your choice — sessions are shaped around what resonates with you.

What happens in a first session?

We'll explore what's bringing you to therapy, your goals, and your history, so we can shape a plan that fits you going forward.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, according to the cancellation policy below and shown at booking on Calendly.

What if I'm in crisis?

These sessions are not emergency or crisis services. If you're at risk of immediate harm, please contact local emergency services rather than waiting for an appointment.

Do I need to fill anything out before booking?

Yes — every "Book this session" button opens a short Intake & Consent form first. Once you submit it, you're taken straight to Calendly to pick your slot.

New Here?

Not sure yet what you need — and that's completely okay.

If you're still figuring out what therapy even means for you, unsure which session fits your situation, or just curious to understand your own mind a little better — this is the place to start. We'll figure out the rest together in our first conversation.
